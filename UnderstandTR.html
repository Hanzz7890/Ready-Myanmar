Why do the effects vary?

The same type of hazard can affect communities differently. Consider the people and buildings present, their vulnerability and the resources available to respond.

Hazard
A process or event that can cause harm, such as ground shaking.
Exposure
People, buildings and other assets located where a hazard may occur.
Vulnerability
Conditions that make people or places more likely to be harmed.
Preparedness
Knowledge, plans and arrangements that help people respond to an emergency.
